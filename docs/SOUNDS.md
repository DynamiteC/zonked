# The sounds, in plain words

Everything is made live in your browser from math. Zonked never downloads audio.
Zonked is not a medical device. It may help some people relax or focus. It does not treat or cure anything.

| Sound | What it is | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Lo-fi | Warm, slow chords with a tape and vinyl feel, like a quiet study radio. Four chords loop slowly, and the tape wobbles a little. | preference |
| Soft piano | A soft piano playing gentle, slow phrases with long ringing notes. A pedal wash makes the notes blur softly. | preference |
| Violet hiss | A thin, bright hiss, like gentle static. Keep it quiet. Its level is capped for your ears. | limited |
| Rain hush | A steady rain-like hush, even across the pitches (pink noise). | mixed |
| Deep rumble | A deep, low rumble like distant thunder or a big waterfall (brown noise). | preference |
| Soft chimes | Soft bells that ring now and then at uneven gaps. | preference |
| Beats only | A soft pulse you hear as two slightly different tones, one per ear. Use headphones. | mixed |

Slow movement: layers swell and fade like a breath (every 5 to 20 seconds), can spread wider across both ears, and can drift slowly left and right. All of it only turns sounds down, never up.

Safety limits that never change: carrier 20-1500 Hz, beat 0.5-40 Hz, master gain at most 0.85, output hard-limited to 0.95.
See `docs/RESEARCH.md` for the studies behind the band guide.
