# Zonked Library: Project Brief (verbatim decisions, do not ask back)

Branch `feature/zonked-library` from `main`. Execute end to end, no intermediate confirmations, until built, tested, audited, documented, ready to merge.

1. 210+ templates from a deterministic DSL in `web/src/catalog/`. Categories: vibes, healing, lucid-dreams, meditation, sleep, adhd-control, focus, work-recovery. Seed from the 21 Android programs and 3 Gnaural schedules in `legacy/`, plus curated protocols (ADHD steadiness, sleep onset, lucid dreaming, deep meditation, healing, AI-coding burnout recovery).
2. Layers are synthesized only, inside `crates/dsp-core` (`#![no_std]`, no heap in render, O(1) per frame): lo-fi (warm bed, tape saturation, sparse crackle), single instrument (plucks and drones), multi-instrument (chordal pads), chimes (bell partials, decay), snare and percussion (filtered noise plus pitched transient). No MP3 or OGG downloads. First-load JS under 120 KB gzip via lazy chunks.
3. Mascot gets an interactive 3D tilt, parallax and depth effect (pointer, drag, hover, subtle audio-reactive depth). Eyes keep pulsing at fL and fR. `Reduce motion` and `prefers-reduced-motion` freeze the tilt.
4. Preserve CSP (`script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`), COOP same-origin, COEP require-corp, CORP same-origin, nosniff, no-referrer, same-origin assets only, hearing safety (carrier 20-1500, beat 0.5-40, gain <= 0.85, 0.95 brickwall across all summed layers). Add a bundle budget check and a host dry-run check.
5. Recommender: pure deterministic `recommendTemplates(context)`. Context: situation (adhd-focus, cant-sleep, lucid-dream, meditate, heal-stress, ai-burnout, deep-work, quick-reset), vibe, availableMinutes, timeOfDay, sensorySensitivity, deviceType (headphones, bluetooth, speaker). Returns at least 5 ranked templates, each with a score and human-readable reasons. Tests prove determinism and >= 5 results for every situation.
6. Timers in player: countdown, sleep fade-out (ramps beat toward low delta/theta and gain down over N minutes, then stops), focus/break Pomodoro, state persisted.
7. Background and lock-screen: visibilitychange keeps audio running, optional Screen Wake Lock, safe AudioContext recovery. MediaSession: play, pause, stop, nexttrack, previoustrack, seekforward, seekbackward, per-template metadata and artwork, setPositionState synced to timers.
8. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, ArrowRight/ArrowLeft or N/P next/prev template, 1-5 launch top-5 recommendations, R recommender, T cycle timer, M mute, +/- gain step within the safe cap, ? help. One table is the single source of truth and renders the help.
9. UI: vibe and situation recommender (>= 5 results with explanations and one-click Play), browser with search and filters (vibe, category, ADHD-friendly, layer tags), layer mixer, timer controls, wake lock toggle, headphone and Bluetooth status. Lazy-load Editor, Catalog and Recommender drawers. Keep PWA offline precache.
10. Verify mixed content: an https page calling `http://localhost:43210`. Chromium and Firefox allow loopback; Safari does not. E2E checks and documents it.
11. Final: Playwright E2E (audio, offline, timers, shortcuts), refresh demo video, deck, HLD, LLD, README, audits. Repo clean on the branch; give merge commands.
